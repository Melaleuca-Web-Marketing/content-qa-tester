    Settings
    Custom page templates

    Must contain @@BANNER_SNIPPET@@. Can use @@BANNER_DESKTOP@@, @@BANNER_MEDIUM@@, @@BANNER_SMALL@@ and <!-- BRAND_CSS --> (same tokens as the VS Code extension's page-context.html).

    Keep-out zones:

    Keep-out zones: drag over parts of the image copy must not cover (products, faces, logos). They also mark where the objects begin for the Center copy check. Copy area (optional, one per image): the exact space to center the copy in. Without one, the space runs from the banner edges to the nearest zone. Start it at the image's edge to mean "the banner edge". Everything is stored relative to the image, so it follows the crop at each width; white dashed outlines show what's visible at the narrowest and widest viewports that use this image.
